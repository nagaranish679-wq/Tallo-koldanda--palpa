Our Local Hero

Remembering the bravery, sacrifice, and local history of Sahid Kewal Gaha Magar.

Sahid Kewal Gaha Magar

Full Name: Kewal Gaha Magar

Title: Sahid / Martyr

Community: Gaha Magar / Magar

Birth Date: 2030 B.S.

Death Date: 2058 B.S.

Village / Tole: Tallo Koldanda

Former Area / Ward: Darlamdanda-4

Local Level: Baganaskali Rural Municipality

District: Palpa

Province: Lumbini Province

Country: Nepal

Nationality: Nepali

Native Place: Tallo Koldanda, Palpa, Nepal

Sahid Kewal Gaha Magar was a respected person from Tallo Koldanda, Palpa. He is remembered as a martyr who sacrificed his life for the country, justice, and the rights of people.

He cared about society and the problems faced by ordinary people. He wanted people to live with freedom, equality, and respect.

Sahid Kewal Gaha Magar raised his voice against injustice and worked for positive change. His courage, dedication, and love for the country made him an inspiration to many.

His contribution continues to be remembered by the local community. The memorial built in his name helps preserve his memory and reminds people of his sacrifice.

His life reminds us to love our country, help our community, respect others, and stand for truth and justice. Sahid Kewal Gaha Magar remains an important part of the history and identity of Tallo Koldanda.

Village Life in Tallo Koldanda

Tallo Koldanda is a village in Palpa, Nepal, where community, culture, and nature are important parts of everyday life.

Culture and Traditions

Local festivals, traditional music, dance, customs, and cultural celebrations help preserve the identity and traditions of the community.

Natural Beauty

The surrounding hills, greenery, fresh air, and peaceful landscapes make Tallo Koldanda a special place for people who appreciate nature and village life.

Community Spirit

Community cooperation and mutual support bring people together to celebrate important occasions and contribute to the development of the village.

Our Village, Our Pride

Tallo Koldanda represents our home, heritage, and community. Preserving its history and traditions helps future generations understand and appreciate their roots.
